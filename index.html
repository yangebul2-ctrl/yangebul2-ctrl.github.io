Privacy Policy for Sue Your Parents

Last Updated: August 13, 2026

Sue Your Parents respects your privacy.

Data Collection

Sue Your Parents does not collect personal information.

No user account is required. The app does not ask for a name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or other personally identifiable information.

Advertising and Tracking

No advertising or advertising tracking is used.

The app does not track users across apps or websites.

Analytics

No analytics SDK that collects personal information is used.

The app does not include crash-reporting or diagnostic SDKs that collect personal information.

Local Game Data

Game progress and settings are stored locally on the device so the game can continue where you left off.

Local game data is not transmitted to the developer or to a remote server.

You can erase local data with the in-app reset control or by deleting the app.

Sharing

No personal data is sold or shared.

Because personal data is not collected, the developer has no remotely stored personal data to delete.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

Policy changes will be reflected on this published page with a new last-updated date.

Contact

Users may contact yangebul2@gmail.com with privacy questions.
