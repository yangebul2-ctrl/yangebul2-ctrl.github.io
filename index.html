Privacy Policy for Sue Your Parents

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

Sue Your Parents respects your privacy.

Data Collection

Sue Your Parents does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The app does not require users to create an account or provide a name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or other personally identifiable information.

Advertising and Tracking

Sue Your Parents does not contain advertising and does not use advertising trackers.

The app does not track users across apps or websites.

Analytics

The app does not use analytics services to collect or transmit personal information or gameplay behavior to the developer.

Local Game Data

Game progress or settings may be stored locally on the user's device when necessary for gameplay.

This local information is not transmitted to the developer or to a remote server.

Third Parties

Sue Your Parents does not sell or share personal information with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the app's privacy practices change in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect those changes.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the developer at yangebul2@gmail.com, or through the support contact listed on the App Store product page.
